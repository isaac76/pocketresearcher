# Question 1: No Largest Natural Number

**Statement:** Prove that there is no largest natural number.

**Formal Statement:** For all natural numbers n, there exists a natural number m such that m > n.

**Instructions:**
Using only the axioms and proof methods provided to you, construct a proof that demonstrates there cannot be a largest natural number. Be explicit about which axioms you use and which proof method you apply.

**Hint:** Consider what properties of natural numbers allow us to always find a larger number.
