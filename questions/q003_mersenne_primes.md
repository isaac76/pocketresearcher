# Question 3: Infinitely Many Mersenne Primes

**Statement:** Prove that there are infinitely many Mersenne prime numbers.

**Background:** A Mersenne prime is a prime number of the form M_p = 2^p - 1, where p is a prime number. Examples include 3 (2^2 - 1), 7 (2^3 - 1), and 31 (2^5 - 1). Whether there are infinitely many Mersenne primes is one of the greatest unsolved problems in number theory.
